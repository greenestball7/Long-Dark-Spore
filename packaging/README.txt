Spore: The Long Dark (FrostSpore) 0.1.0
=======================================

Spore's cell and creature stages on a frozen world with The Long Dark's survival rules and sound.

Play it from Melty: press Play. Melty starts FrostSpore.exe with your Spore and The Long Dark folders;
it reads The Long Dark's own audio from your install (nothing from either game is shipped here),
then starts Spore Galactic Adventures with the mod loaded.

Needs: Spore with Galactic Adventures (Steam, GOG or EA app) and The Long Dark, both installed.

In game
- Warmth, Calories (cell + creature), Thirst and Fatigue (creature) bars, bottom left.
- Feels-like temperature = air + wind chill + shelter. Below zero your warmth drains; at zero you
  lose 20% health per in-game hour (40% once hypothermia sets in).
- Weather cycles between clear, light snow and blizzards (whiteout, much colder).
- Warm up: eat (cell), stand at your nest or huddle with your herd (creature), keep moving.
- Drink by standing in water; rest by standing still or at your nest.
- One in-game hour = 40 real seconds.

Logs: %LOCALAPPDATA%\FrostSpore\logs (launcher.log, mod.log)
